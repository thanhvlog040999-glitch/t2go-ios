APP T2GO CHO IPHONE - CACH LAY FILE IPA VA CAI VAO MAY
======================================================

App nay la ban boc trang t2liveai.io.vn giong het app Android:
 dang nhap Google o lai trong app, goi thoai/video dung camera + micro,
 vuot trai/phai de quay lai, chon anh/video dang bai binh thuong.

DONG BO DU LIEU: app khong chua du lieu rieng - moi thu (tai khoan, bai
 dang, tin nhan, ban be, xu, trang trai) nam tren may chu t2liveai.io.vn,
 nen dang nhap cung 1 tai khoan tren web, Android hay iPhone thi thay giong
 het nhau, sua ben nay ben kia cap nhat ngay. Dang nhap duoc giu lai sau
 moi lan mo app (khong phai dang nhap lai). Mat mang thi app hien trang
 bao loi co nut Thu lai; tep tai ve trong trang se tai xuong va mo chia se.

BUOC 1 - DUNG FILE IPA (khong can may Mac, mien phi):
 1. Tao tai khoan GitHub (github.com) neu chua co - mien phi.
 2. Tao repo moi (New repository), tai het cac file trong thu muc nay len
    (ke ca thu muc .github - tren web GitHub bam "uploading an existing file"
     roi keo tha toan bo; neu kho day .github len thi tao file truc tiep:
     Add file > Create new file, go duong dan .github/workflows/build-ipa.yml
     roi dan noi dung tuong ung).
 3. Vao tab Actions cua repo: ban dung chay tu dong (may Mac cua GitHub dung
    khoang 3-5 phut). Xong bam vao ban dung > tai file T2GO-ipa ve (la file
    T2GO.ipa ben trong).

BUOC 2 - CAI VAO IPHONE:
 - May iOS 16 (16.0 - 16.6.1): cai TrollStore truoc (lien he Muse de lay
   cach cai dung voi dung phien ban), mo TrollStore > dau + > chon T2GO.ipa
   > Install. Xong la dung vinh vien, khong het han.
 - May iOS 18: cai SideStore (ghep noi may tinh 1 lan dau), mo SideStore >
   dau + > chon T2GO.ipa > cai. SideStore tu gia han nen app song lien tuc,
   gia han hoan toan mien phi.

Luu y: file IPA nay chua ky san (unsigned) - TrollStore/SideStore se tu ky
bang tai khoan cua ban khi cai, nhu vay moi dung duoc va khong ton tien.
